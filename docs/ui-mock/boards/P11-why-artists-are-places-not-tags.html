An artist is a place, not a tag
YorushikaYOASOBInight drivechill
The name is tappable wherever it appears and opens the artist's page. In the Add sheet, artists are a section beside tags, so 'Chopin + Mozart' is two taps. The chip wears a small figure where a tag wears its dot. Artists never show in the Tags list, on Home tiles or in anything the app calls a tag, and cannot be renamed or deleted. The bridge: an artist page's ⋯ offers 'Make a tag from this artist'. It runs once, says what it did, and the tag is yours to grow from then on.
A tag named like an artist: nudge, then allow
New tag: "yorushika"Yorushika is already an artist here, with their 20 songs. A tag is for songs you choose, whoever made them.
Open the artistMake the tag anyway
Most people wanted the artist and get there in one tap. Anyone who insists still gets the tag, and the two never look alike: figure against dot, separate sections, only the tag in the Tags list. The same nudge runs the other way when the bridge would collide with a tag you already have: it opens that tag.
