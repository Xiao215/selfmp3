Why

One app, laid out by its width.

The widths an iPad Pro 11-inch gives the app, in points, and each width where the layout gives way. The line is the window, not the screen.

The width decides, never the device.An iPad is a phone in Slide Over, a phone a little wider in Split View, and a small computer full screen. Nothing asks what the device is to choose a layout.
Except the words.A sentence names the device: "on this iPad", "tells this iPad where it is". An iPad is wide enough for a computer’s layout and is still not a computer.
A finger, always.Every target is 44 or more, and nothing waits for a hover: no select circle in the number column, no play over a cover. A trackpad pointer hovers, but controls stay visible and finger-sized.
No shortcuts yet.No ⌘K hints and no Shortcuts in Settings until a hardware keyboard is supported (deferred).
Turning keeps your place.Rotating, or dragging the Split View divider across 820, changes the layout under the same page: the song, the scroll, a half-typed search and an open sheet stay.
