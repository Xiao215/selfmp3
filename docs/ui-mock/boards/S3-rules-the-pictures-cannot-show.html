Rules the pictures cannot show

Song rows·One row component everywhere: cover, title, artist with the on-device mark, time, ⋯.·Tags show on a row only in Library, Search and the queue. Inside a tag, an artist or a playlist they are left off.·More tags than fit: show two and a count.·On-device mark: green check when the file is here, grey cloud when it is not. Web shows neither.·The playing row gets a wash in the cover's colour, an equaliser over its cover, and a tinted title.
Tags and artists·Tapping a tag anywhere opens its page. 'Add' on that page opens the sheet that combines tags and artists; every one you turn on adds its songs.·An artist is a place and a chip with a figure. It never appears in the Tags list, on Home tiles, or anywhere the word 'tag' is used, and cannot be renamed or deleted.·Making a tag named exactly like an artist shows a nudge ('already an artist, open it?') and then allows it.·The artist page's ⋯ offers 'Make a tag from this artist'; it runs once.·Two songs with no tag yet: a card at the top of the Tags page. There is no Untagged page.
Up next·Playing a tag, playlist, artist or the library replaces the queue with that list.·After that it belongs to nothing: no 'playing from', no sections.·Editing the queue never changes a tag or a playlist.·Played songs sink to the end, greyed, tappable.·Phone: opens from the mini player's queue button (and the same button in Now Playing) as a sheet that covers the mini player and the bar. Hold to move, swipe left to remove. No grips, no ×.·Computer: a rail. Drag to reorder; drag a row out of the rail to remove, with Undo. Delete and right-click also work and draw nothing.
Search·One Search page. Home's field, the search circle, Library's field, ⌘K and the computer's list-header field all open it.·The door only sets the starting scope: All from Home, Songs from Library, Tags from Tags.·Before typing: your tags and what you played last. No search history.·Results: artists and tags first, then songs, then lyric lines.
Import·Paste a link, or share one to the app, or use the extension. The next step is always the review list.·Every song is coming in unless left out. No checkmarks. A left-out song is dimmed, struck through and says so.·Title and artist are editable per song. There is no album field.·Preview has a bar you can drag. Phone: the row opens in place. Computer: play appears over the cover under the pointer, and the bar opens under the row, aligned to the columns.·Importing only ever tags; it never offers a playlist.
Everything else·Welcome: a first launch shows tag tiles with invented names; a device that has signed in before shows its own last covers. One button. Sign out returns here.·First sync: keep-every-song is off by default on a phone, on by default on a computer.·Playlists sort by last played. No pins. An empty playlist is never listed.·Now Playing: ⓘ opens the song's page; lyrics is a swipe or the pill; songs without lyrics show Horizon or Ripples, two new looks for the existing song visual, coloured from the cover.·The month as a page: five looks to pick from; a computer adds the front page.·Reduce Motion makes every transition instant.
