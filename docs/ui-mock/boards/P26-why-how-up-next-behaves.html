The model Playing a tag, a playlist, an artist or the library replaces the queue with that list. After that it is just 'the queue': no 'playing from', no sections. Reorder it, drop songs, add songs from any ⋯ with 'Add to queue'. What has played sinks to the bottom in grey; tap one to hear it again. Editing the queue never touches a tag or a playlist.
The way in The queue button on the mini player, or a pull up from the foot of the screen. The sheet covers the mini player and the tab bar completely, because while it is open it is the player: the playing song sits at the top with its equaliser, what is next below it, what has played greyed at the end. Hold a song to move it; swipe it left to remove it. No grips, no ×. The same button sits at the foot of Now Playing. On a computer it is a rail beside the list that stays open.
