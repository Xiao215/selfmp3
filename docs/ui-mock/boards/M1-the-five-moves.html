1 · Press Everything you can tap sinks to 0.96 under the finger and springs back. Tiles, rows, buttons, chips: one rule, one spring.
2 · Play becomes pause The glyph turns and scales through the swap, 180 ms, with a light tap of haptics. The button itself never moves.
3 · The mini player arrives First song of the session: the card rises from under the tab bar with a small overshoot, 320 ms. Songs after that only crossfade the words.
4 · Tiles arrive On the first paint of Home only: tiles fade up 60 ms apart. Never again on a tab switch, so it stays a welcome and not a wait.
5 · The cover breathes In Now Playing the cover shrinks to 0.84 while paused and grows back on play, 400 ms. The page says whether it is playing without you reading a glyph.
One spring for the whole app Today: fixed timings of 100, 140 and 220 ms with an ease-out. Proposed: keep those for fades, and add one spring token (stiffness 220, damping 24) for anything that moves in space: presses, the rise, the sheet, the swap. With Reduce Motion on, every one of these is instant, as now. The cover-to-Now-Playing shared transition is a possible sixth move; it is the only one that needs new machinery, so it is not in this set.
