Three rules before any of theseIt works on your library only; it never recommends music you do not own. It proposes and you approve; nothing in the library changes behind your back. It is never a chat window: each idea is one button in a place that already exists, and the app is complete with all of them off.
A1 · Describe a playlist'Slow, rainy, mostly piano, nothing I played this week.' It builds a normal playlist from your own songs using tempo, energy, lyrics and your tags. This is the 'Smart' playlist you already wanted, with words instead of templates.Where it lives: New playlist › Describe it. The result is an ordinary playlist you can edit.
A2 · What is this line sayingTap a lyric line for a translation and a short note on the idiom or the reference. Made for a library that is half Japanese; sits beside the Romaji you already have.Where it lives: A tap on a line in Lyrics. Off until the first tap.
A3 · Find it by what you remember'The one about a night train', 'that live version with the long intro'. Search by meaning across lyrics, titles and your own notes, not just matching letters.Where it lives: The same Search page; a 'By meaning' scope beside Lyrics.
A4 · Tidy up, with a reviewヨルシカ and Yorushika are one artist. 'Disc 1 Glazed Moon…｜Genshin Impact' is a title that swallowed its album. It proposes merges and cleaner names as a list of before and after; you tick what is right.Where it lives: Settings › Library › Tidy up. Nothing applies until you press Apply.
A5 · Your month, writtenThe 'Words' poster, but actually written for you each month: one warm sentence or two drawn from your numbers, never generic.Where it lives: The month as a page, as the Words look.
A6 · Practice helperSplit vocals from the band so you can sing or play along; mark verse, chorus and bridge on the scrubber; suggest the chords. Runs on your computer, slowly, once per song.Where it lives: Now Playing › Practice, which already exists for speed and loops.
If only one ships first: A4. It fixes a mess every imported library has, it is the least 'AI-looking', and the review list makes it completely safe. A1 is the one people would show a friend.
