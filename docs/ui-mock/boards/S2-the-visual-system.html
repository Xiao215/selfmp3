The accent is the user's own hue (Settings); 268° is the default. No hairline borders anywhere: separation is tone on tone. The playing colour has no fixed value: each song takes the main hue of its own cover, a light version for the title and equaliser and a mid one for the wash behind the row, the player bar and the mini player. A cover with no real colour falls back to the accent. The app already picks this colour (useSongColor, docs/features/now-playing-colour.md): keep that, and carry it to the new mini player and queue. The green in these mocks is only what one song's cover gives.
