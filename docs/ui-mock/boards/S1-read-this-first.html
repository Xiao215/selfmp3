Read this first.

Every screen here was reviewed and agreed, one round at a time, in September 2026. Nothing on these pages is an open option unless its title says so. Build what you see; the notes beside each row say what is not obvious from the picture.
The ideaThe app keeps your own music and lets you find it by the tags you give it. The redesign makes tags places you go, not filters you hunt for, and makes every screen calm: tone on tone, no hairlines, pills instead of rectangles, one serif voice for greetings and big numbers.
How the canvas is laid out·1 · Start here: this page, the visual system, and the rules the pictures cannot show.·2 · Phone: every phone screen, in the order a person meets them.·3 · Computer and web: the same, at desktop width.·4 · Extension, 5 · Motion, 6 · Later: the browser extension, how things move, and ideas that are not scheduled.
How to read a board·Boards marked with a play button are linked: press Play and tap through the flow.·Data is real: covers, titles and tags come from the development library.·Anything drawn mid-gesture (a row half swiped, a row lifted) is showing the gesture, not a permanent state.·The blue note beside each row is the spec for that row.
Where things live
Phone·A floating bar: Home · Library · Playlists, and a separate search circle.·You is the avatar in Home's header: the month, Import, Report, Settings.·Import is also the + in Home's header.·A mini player floats above the bar whenever a song is loaded; its queue button opens Up next.·Tags open from Home (tiles, then 'All tags'). They are never a tab and never under You.
Computer and web·A sidebar: Search, Home, Library, Import, then Playlists and Tags as sections whose headers open the full list, then your name, then Settings last.·A player bar across the foot, absent while nothing is loaded: speed, volume, Up next, devices.·Up next is a rail beside the list that stays open.·Web is the same layout in a tab: no menu bar, no ⌘K, Space for play and pause, and no on-device marks because a browser streams.
What changed from today's app·Landing page is Home, not the flat Library.·Tabs were Library · Playlists · Import · You; now Home · Library · Playlists plus search.·Tag chips lost their saturated fills: a neutral pill with a hue dot; selected is white.·A tag, an artist and a playlist all open as the same kind of page.·Artists are places and chips with a figure, never tags.·One search everywhere; where you open it only sets the starting scope.·The queue is editable and belongs to nothing: playing a list replaces it.·Untagged page, pinned playlists, Shuffle-all on Home, the sign-in page and the song menu's Play next and Select are gone.
