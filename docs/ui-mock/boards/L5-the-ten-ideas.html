Treasure · things that make it yours
T1 · A note on a song'First heard at the festival in Osaka.' One line of your own on any song, dated. Searchable. The thing a streaming service can never hold for you.Where it lives: The song's page, under 'You and this song'.
T2 · On this day'A year ago today you added 晴る.' One quiet line on Home when there is something to say, nothing when there is not.Where it lives: Home, in the greeting's second line.
T3 · Is my music safe?One screen that says where your library lives and how many copies exist: this Mac, your storage, your phone. Green when there are two or more, a nudge when there is one.Where it lives: Settings › Library, and a line on You.
T4 · A better copyShows each file's quality in plain words. When you get a better rip, drop it on the song and it replaces the file while keeping plays, tags, notes and lyrics.Where it lives: The song's page › About. Drag and drop on a computer.
Listening · quality you feel, settings you never see
L1 · Even loudness, no gapsSongs from different sources play at the same level, and live albums and classical movements run into each other without a click.Where it lives: On by default. One switch in Settings › Playback.
L2 · Going offline'I'm flying on Friday.' Pick tags and it keeps those plus your most played on the phone, shows the size first, and cleans up when you are back.Where it lives: Settings › On this phone › Going offline.
L3 · Wake to a tagAn alarm that starts a tag quietly and rises over two minutes. The sleep timer's mirror image.Where it lives: Now Playing › Sleep becomes Sleep and wake.
L4 · In the car and on the wristCarPlay and a watch app that show your tags as the first screen, because in a car you choose a mood, not a song.Where it lives: Outside the app entirely.
Getting music in
I1 · Your purchases and your discsBandcamp purchases by signing in once; a CD by inserting it. Both arrive tagged with where they came from.Where it lives: Import, as two more rows under the paste box.
I2 · Hand a friend a mixtapeShare a playlist as a page of covers, titles and your notes. No files leave your library; they see what to look for.Where it lives: A playlist's ⋯ › Share as a page.
